Many patients from Nigeria travel to India for surgery and long-term care. This guide covers the main steps, from sending your first reports to flying home, so you know what to prepare and who to ask.

Step 1: Share your reports and compare hospitals

Start with your medical reports and scans. Send them to Shifara Health on WhatsApp or through our free consultation form. Our medical team reviews your case and shares opinions and written cost estimates from up to 3 accredited hospitals. Most patients hear back within 48 hours of sharing complete reports.

You can also browse on your own:

  • Best hospitals in India, by city and speciality
  • Best doctors in India, with their qualifications and experience
  • Medical treatment in India, with indicative costs

Step 2: Understand what treatment will cost

Each treatment page shows an indicative package price in US dollars, and our treatment cost comparison by country shows how India compares with other destinations. Your final figure comes from a written estimate after the hospital reviews your reports.

Shifara Health does not charge patients. You pay the hospital directly, as per the written estimate. Remember to budget for flights, your stay near the hospital and everyday expenses as well.

Step 3: Apply for your medical visa

Patients travelling for treatment need a medical visa, not a tourist or business visa. Nationals of many countries in Africa can apply online for the e-Medical Visa. The eligible list changes, so check it on the official website, indianvisaonline.gov.in, or ask us. If your country is not on the list, you apply for a regular Medical Visa at the Indian High Commission.

For the e-Medical Visa, apply at least 4 days before you arrive. You will need:

  • A passport valid for at least 6 months from arrival, with at least 2 blank pages
  • A recent passport-size photo with a white background, and a scan of your passport's photo page
  • An invitation letter from the hospital in India, on its letterhead
  • Recent medical reports supporting the need for treatment
  • A card to pay the visa fee online

We arrange the hospital's invitation letter and help you with the form, free. Up to 2 family members can travel with you on attendant visas. Read the full India medical visa guide for details.

Step 4: What happens after you land

Your case manager stays with you from the airport to the day you fly home. As part of our medical tourism service, we help with:

  • Airport pickup, and an ambulance for patients who need one
  • A guest house, serviced apartment or hotel close to the hospital
  • Language support at consultations and in hospital, where available
  • An Indian SIM card and exchanging money at authorised exchanges

Step 5: Recovery and follow-up at home

If your doctor wants you to stay close before you fly, we can arrange step-down care after discharge. Nurses and caregivers are available during treatment and recovery. Once you are home, we schedule your follow-up, and a video consultation lets you speak with your specialist without travelling again.

If you want to speak to someone first, a Shifara case manager can answer your questions before you commit to anything.
