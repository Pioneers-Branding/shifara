Which visa do I need?

Medical Visa Types for India

Patients travelling for treatment need a medical visa, not a tourist or business visa. Each family member travelling with the patient needs an attendant visa.

e-Medical Visa

For the patient · Apply online

Applied for online, with no embassy visit. It can be extended in some cases through the FRRO if treatment takes longer.

  • StayUp to 60 days
  • EntriesUp to 3
  • ApplyOnline

e-Medical Attendant Visa

For family members · Apply online

For family members travelling with an e-Medical Visa patient. Its validity is linked to the patient's visa.

  • WhoFamily members
  • NumberUp to 2
  • ApplyOnline

Medical Visa (regular)

For the patient · Embassy or IVAC

Applied for through the Indian Embassy, High Commission or an Indian Visa Application Centre. For nationals not eligible for the e-Visa, or for longer treatment plans.

  • StayPer treatment plan
  • EntriesUp to 3
  • ApplyIn person

Medical Attendant Visa (regular)

For family members · Embassy or IVAC

For family members travelling with a regular Medical Visa patient, applied for at the same mission as the patient.

  • WhoFamily members
  • NumberUsually up to 2
  • ApplyIn person

Document checklist

What You Need to Apply

Patient

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months from arrival, with at least 2 blank pages
  • Recent passport-size photo with a white background (digital copy)
  • Scan of the passport's photo (bio-data) page
  • Invitation letter from the hospital in India, on its letterhead
  • Recent medical reports supporting the need for treatment
  • A card for paying the visa fee online

Attendant

  • Passport, photo and bio-data page scan, as above
  • The patient's visa application or visa number
  • Attendant's name included in the hospital's invitation letter
Applying from your country

Where to Apply

Bangladesh

Bangladesh is not on the e-Visa list. Patients apply for a regular Medical Visa through an Indian Visa Application Centre (IVAC). We share the hospital letter and help you prepare the file.

Africa and the Middle East

Nationals of many countries in Africa and the Middle East can apply online for the e-Medical Visa. The eligible list changes, so check it on the official e-Visa website or ask us. If your country is not on it, apply for a regular Medical Visa at the Indian Embassy or High Commission.

Use only official websites. The e-Visa is issued at indianvisaonline.gov.in. Other sites that look official may charge extra fees. Shifara Health does not charge for visa help.

In India

After You Arrive

Your case manager stays with you from the airport to the day you fly home.

Airport pickup

Our team meets you at arrivals and takes you to the hospital or your stay. Ambulance pickup for patients who need it.

Registration (FRRO)

Regular Medical Visa holders usually register with the FRRO / FRO within 14 days of arrival, online at indianfrro.gov.in. We help with the form.

Visa extension

If treatment takes longer than planned, apply for an extension online through the FRRO before your visa ends, with a letter from the treating hospital.

Stay near the hospital

Guest houses, serviced apartments and hotels close to the hospital, for the patient and attendants.

Interpreters

Arabic, French, Bengali, Russian and other language support at consultations and in hospital.

Local SIM and money

Help getting an Indian SIM card (passport and visa needed) and exchanging currency at authorised exchanges.

Before you fly

Travel Checklist

  • Printed visa (ETA), passport and hospital letter in your hand luggage
  • All medical reports, scans and prescriptions, on paper and on your phone
  • Enough of your regular medicines, with the prescription
  • Flight details shared with your case manager for airport pickup
  • Money for the first days, and a card that works abroad
  • Copies of passports and visas, kept separately from the originals
  • Comfortable clothes for the hospital and the season
