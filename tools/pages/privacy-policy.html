3. Why we use your data

We process your personal data only on the basis of your consent, given through the consent box on our forms or when you contact us, and only for these purposes:

  • to understand your medical requirement and prepare treatment options and cost estimates;
  • to share your case with the hospitals and doctors you choose, so they can give an opinion and estimate;
  • to contact you about your enquiry by phone, WhatsApp or email;
  • if you decide to travel, to help with visa invitation letters, travel, stay and post-treatment follow-up;
  • to meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use your health information for marketing.

4. Who we share it with

  • Hospitals and doctors you choose: to review your case, give a medical opinion and an estimate.
  • Service providers acting on our behalf (Data Processors), such as email, cloud storage and CRM providers, under written contracts that require them to protect your data. [List main processors, e.g. Google Workspace, Zoho CRM]
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it (for example, for medical visa processing).

Hospitals and doctors are separate Data Fiduciaries for the care they provide to you and handle your data under their own privacy policies.

5. Transfers outside India

Because our patients live outside India, your data will be exchanged with you in your country. Our systems are hosted in [India / country of hosting]. Any transfer outside India is made in line with the DPDP Act and any restrictions notified by the Government of India.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your personal data only as long as needed for the purpose you gave it for. If you do not proceed with treatment, we erase your data [e.g. 12 months] after our last contact with you, unless you ask us to erase it sooner or the law requires us to keep it longer. Before erasing data because you have been inactive, we will tell you at least 48 hours in advance so you can contact us if you still need our help.

7. How we protect it

We use reasonable security safeguards, including access controls, encryption in transit, limited staff access on a need-to-know basis and logging of access. If a personal data breach occurs, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as required by law.

8. Children

If the patient is under 18, the enquiry must be made by a parent or lawful guardian, who gives consent on the child's behalf. We may ask for reasonable proof of guardianship before processing a child's health information. We do not track children or send them targeted advertising.

9. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it. Withdrawal does not affect processing already done.
  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with.
  • Correct, complete or update your data.
  • Erase your data when it is no longer needed for the purpose you gave it for.
  • Nominate a person to exercise these rights for you in case of death or incapacity.
  • Raise a grievance with us and, if you are not satisfied, with the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email mail@shifarahealth.com or message us on WhatsApp with the subject "Privacy request". We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.
