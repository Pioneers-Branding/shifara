Our story

A Medical Care Companion Across India

Travelling abroad for treatment brings questions at every step: which hospital, which doctor, what it will cost, how to get a visa, where to stay. Shifara Health was set up to answer those questions for international patients and their families.

We connect patients with accredited hospitals, specialists and support services in India's major medical cities, and stay with them from the first message through treatment, recovery and follow-up care.

Seeking care far from home takes courage. It helps to have someone who knows the way and holds your hand through each step. Shifara is that hand.

The right partner in your healing journey.
