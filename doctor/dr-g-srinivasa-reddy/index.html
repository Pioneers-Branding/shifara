About the Doctor

Dr. G. Srinivasa Reddy is an ENT surgeon whose practice includes endoscopic sinus surgery, micro-ear surgery, and airway disorders. With over 22 years of surgical experience, he treats patients with chronic sinusitis, hearing loss, and voice disorders, and offers minimal-access treatment options where appropriate.

Areas of Expertise

Endoscopic Sinus & Skull Base Surgery (FESS)

Endoscopic treatment for chronic sinusitis, nasal polyps, and CSF rhinorrhoea.

Micro-Ear Surgery & Hearing Care

Tympanoplasty, mastoidectomy, and stapedectomy for chronic ear infections and conductive hearing loss.

Laryngology & Voice Disorders

Microlaryngeal surgery for vocal cord polyps, nodules, and hoarseness.

Paediatric ENT Surgery

Minimally invasive adenoidectomy, tonsillectomy, and grommet insertion for middle-ear effusion.

Key Achievements

  • Has treated more than 15,000 outpatient and surgical ENT cases over two decades of clinical practice.
  • Uses coblation technology in tonsil and adenoid surgeries.
  • Regular participant and faculty member at endoscopic sinus surgery workshops.

Awards & Recognition

  • Life Member of the Association of Otolaryngologists of India (AOI).
  • Conferred with academic distinction during postgraduate ENT training.
  • Practice focus on minimal-access endoscopic sinus care.
