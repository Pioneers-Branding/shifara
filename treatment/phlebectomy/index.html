What is Phlebectomy?

Phlebectomy is a minimally invasive procedure used to remove selected varicose veins through very small incisions in the skin.

It may be recommended for superficial veins that are causing symptoms or where a specialist determines removal is appropriate. The procedure can be performed as a standalone treatment or as part of a broader vein treatment plan, depending on the patient’s condition.

Why opt for Phlebectomy in India?

India offers access to experienced vascular specialists and modern minimally invasive vein treatments without the prolonged waiting periods often experienced elsewhere.
