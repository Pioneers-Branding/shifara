About the Hospital

Yatharth Super Speciality Hospital, Noida, is a multi-specialty institution providing tertiary healthcare. Housing more than 30 clinical departments under one roof, the hospital combines modern medical technology with a patient-first approach. Yatharth treats complex conditions in cardiac sciences, renal transplants, and laparoscopic surgery. The hospital features highly specialized amenities for international travelers, facilitating coordinated treatment pathways for patients from around the globe.

Key Highlights

  • Tertiary care hospital with 250+ beds and 81 dedicated critical care beds
  • NABH accredited healthcare facility
  • Core expertise in Interventional Neurosciences, Renal Sciences, Orthopedics, and Cancer Care
  • Advanced medical setups including a 128-Slice CT Scan, 1.5 Tesla MRI, and modern Cath Labs
  • Robotic-assisted surgeries and gastrointestinal oncology interventions
  • Modular operation theaters with dedicated endoscopy units
  • Complete end-to-end international patient management, in-house interpreters, and financial counseling
