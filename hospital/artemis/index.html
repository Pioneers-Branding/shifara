About the Hospital

Artemis Hospitals, established in 2007, is a multi-super specialty healthcare venture launched by the promoters of the Apollo Tyres Group. Spread across a 9-acre campus with a capacity of over 750 beds, Artemis is a tertiary care hospital in North India. It holds JCI and NABH accreditations. It has over 400 full-time physicians and specialists across 14 Centres of Excellence, and treats domestic and international patients.

International Patient Services

Artemis Hospital Group provides the following services for international patients:

Visa and Travel Arrangement

Medical visa invitation letters, remote pre-departure tele-consultations, cost estimates, and complimentary airport pick-up and drop-off services from New Delhi.

Language Interpretation Services

On-site language translators for patients from multiple regions.

Accommodation Assistance

Coordination of hospital admissions, with booking assistance for nearby hotels, serviced apartments, and long-stay suites.

In-Hospital Guest Comforts

Access to an International Patient Lounge, a 24/7 cafeteria serving international cuisines, and prayer rooms.

Concierge Services

On-site travel desks and authorised money exchange services, with 24/7 international helpline access and patient companions for post-discharge follow-up.
