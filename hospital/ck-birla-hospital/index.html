About the Hospital

CK Birla Hospital, Gurgaon, located in Block J, Mayfield Garden, Sector 51, is a multi-specialty hospital of the CK Birla Group. Built around international UK NHS guidelines and patient-centered clinical care, the 100+ bedded hospital follows defined clinical safety protocols and has modern surgical infrastructure. Driven by over 70 internationally trained consultants and multidisciplinary boards, CK Birla Hospital features specialized centers for Women’s Health, Oncology, Orthopaedics, Minimal Access Surgery, and Paediatrics, serving domestic and international patients.

Key Highlights

Hospital Campus

A modern 100+ bedded multi-specialty facility featuring executive inpatient suites, advanced critical care beds, and modular operation theaters.

UK NHS Standard Protocols

Operating protocols designed in alignment with UK NHS clinical governance and infection control benchmarks.

Birthing & Gynaecology Unit

Houses modern Labor Delivery Recovery (LDR) rooms, Level-III NICU suites, and advanced fetal medicine units.

Robotic & Orthopaedic Surgery

Equipped with robotic surgical systems for minimally invasive joint replacements and gynaecological surgeries.

Accreditations

Accredited by NABH and NABL.

Awards & Accolades

  • Economic Times Healthcare Awards: Received the “Best Hospital in Women and Child Care” award.
  • Times Health Survey: Featured in the survey of specialty hospitals in Delhi NCR.
  • AHPI Healthcare Excellence Awards: Recognised in the Nursing Excellence and Patient-Centric Operations categories.

International Patient Services

CK Birla Hospital, Gurgaon, provides services for international patients. These services include:

Visa and Pre-Arrival Support

Generation of formal medical visa invitation letters, remote pre-arrival tele-consultations, and custom financial estimations.

Transit & Airport Pickup Logistics

Complimentary private vehicle transport from Indira Gandhi International Airport to the hospital campus.

Language Interpretation Services

On-site interpreters for several languages.

Accommodation & Inpatient Amenities

Guidance on nearby hotels, long-stay serviced apartments, and in-hospital private recovery suites.

Dedicated Post-Care Desk

Personal guest relations manager, customized dietary choices, local currency exchange, and digital post-discharge tracking.
