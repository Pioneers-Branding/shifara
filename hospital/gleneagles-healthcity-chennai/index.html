About the Hospital

Gleneagles HealthCity (formerly Gleneagles Global Health City), located in Perumbakkam, Chennai, is a 1,000-bed quaternary care multi-specialty hospital with a multi-organ transplant center. Spanning a 21-acre campus, the hospital is part of the IHH Healthcare network. Gleneagles HealthCity performs liver, heart, lung, kidney, and complex neuro-transplant procedures. Equipped with robotic surgical systems, radiation platforms, and critical care units, the hospital receives referrals from domestic and international patients.

Key Highlights

  • Multi-organ transplant center performing adult and pediatric liver, heart, lung, and kidney transplants
  • Accredited facility holding JCI, NABH, NABL, and NABB certifications
  • 21-acre campus housing over 1,000 beds, 300+ ICU beds, and 17 modular operation theaters
  • Technology infrastructure featuring TrueBeam STX Radiotherapy, 3.0 Tesla MRI, 16-Slice PET-CT, and robotic surgery suites
  • Dedicated centers for Medical & Surgical Oncology, Neurosciences, Cardiac Sciences, HPB Surgery, and Orthopedics
  • Integrated 24/7 emergency trauma, air ambulance retrieval network, and interventional Cath Labs
  • International patient lounge offering multi-lingual support, remote clinical reviews, visa facilitation, and accommodation assistance

Awards & Accolades

  • Newsweek Survey – World’s Best Hospitals: Listed in the survey.
  • Times Healthcare Achievers Awards: Received the “Best Multi-Specialty Hospital” and “Excellence in Oncology & Neurosciences” awards (South India).
  • DRI Awards: Received the “Response & Recovery of the Year” award.
  • Sanjeevani Healthcare Expo: Received the “Best Surgical Oncology” award and recognition for its transplant program.
  • India Today Healthcare Awards: Recognised in the Cardiology and Complex Interventions category (South India).

International Patient Services

Gleneagles HealthCity Chennai provides services for international patients:

  • Visa and Travel Arrangement: Medical invitation generation, embassy coordination, custom cost estimations, and complimentary airport pick-up services.
  • Air Ambulance: Dedicated global air evacuation and inter-hospital emergency transport systems.
  • Language Interpretation Services: Interpreters for several languages (Arabic, French, Russian, Swahili, etc.).
  • Accommodation Assistance: Direct assistance with nearby hotels, guest houses, and long-stay serviced apartments near the 21-acre campus.
  • Dedicated International Helpline: 24/7 international patient desk, dedicated guest relation managers, private lounge access, and continuous post-care tracking.
