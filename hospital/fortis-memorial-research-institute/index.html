About the Hospital

Fortis Healthcare Limited is an integrated healthcare delivery service provider in India. Operating an extensive network of 36 healthcare facilities, over 6,000 operational beds, and over 400 diagnostic centers, the group has provided healthcare services for decades. Its facilities range from single-specialty hospitals to multi-super specialty tertiary and quaternary care hospitals, serving domestic and international patients.

Key Highlights

Hospital Network

Operates 36 healthcare facilities with 6,000+ operational beds including multi-specialty and single-specialty referral hospitals.

Accreditations

Includes multiple JCI, NABH, and NABL accredited facilities across the country.

Surgical & Medical Services

Performs robotic-assisted surgeries (including Da Vinci systems), bone marrow and solid organ transplants, beating-heart surgeries, and precision oncology treatments.

Infrastructure
